Your public tournament page

One link. Teams sign themselves up.

  • A page you can send anywhere — text, email, your league's site.
  • Coaches register their team, enter their roster and pay, in one pass.
  • You approve or reject each registration. Nothing gets in behind your back.
  • Dates, venues, categories, entry fee and deadline, all on the page.
4
Entry fees

You keep 100%.

Every dollar a team pays to enter your tournament is a dollar that lands in your account.

0%
platform fee on entry fees
  • No platform fee. Widdo does not take a cut of your entry fees.
  • Stripe Connect payouts go straight to your account, in your name.
  • The money never sits with Widdo. We don't hold it, so we can't be slow returning it.
  • You set the entry fee. Per team, per category, your number.

Card processing is charged by the processor, not by us. Widdo's share of your entry fees is zero.

Scheduling

The schedule that used to take a weekend.

  • Widdo lays out every match across your venues and courts.
  • It respects the constraints you set: rest between games, blocked hours, court availability.
  • Conflicts are flagged before you publish, not by an angry coach after.
  • Move a game and the affected teams get told.
7
Game day

Check in a roster at the table, not in a binder.

  • Scan the QR credential. The roster comes up with photos.
  • Waivers are signed online before anyone travels — you see who's still missing one.
  • Check in a whole team or one player at a time.
  • Missing documents show up as a list, not as a surprise.

Eligibility is settled the week before. Saturday morning is just scanning.
