Payments, rosters, families, tournaments — one platform, AI at the core.

Widdo is the AI-native operating system for sports organizations — any sport, any age, from youth academies to adult leagues. One platform that runs a club's payments, rosters, attendance, families, tournaments and communication, with AI doing the busywork. Built by two basketball lifers — a former professional player and a national-level player — live with clubs in Latin America, selecting U.S. pilot organizations now, and launching across the United States for the 2027 season — every sport.

The problem we solve

Most sports organizations still run on Google Forms, spreadsheets, group chats and chasing payments by hand — and the people running them are coaches, not administrators. Widdo replaces that stack with one platform, and the AI does the chasing, the reporting and the setup, so the owner can coach.

What Widdo does today

Payments & autonomous collections

Stripe-powered billing; the platform chases late payments by itself, politely.

Rosters, attendance & families

Players, categories, sessions, QR check-in, and team messaging that replaces the group chat — in EN/ES/PT.

Tournaments

Registrations, brackets (6 formats), live scoring, online payments — 0% platform fee.

Lifetime athlete profile

The player's sports résumé — stats, history, documents — follows them from club to club, for life.

Public registration & club sites

Shareable enrollment links — no more Google Forms and spreadsheets.

Mobile app

Native app (iOS/Android) for parents, players and coaches, in TestFlight now.

AI-native, not AI-added

The AI isn't a chatbot bolted on — it's how the club actually gets run.

Autonomous collections

The platform detects late payments and follows up with families on its own — the #1 time sink for club owners, gone.

Daily brief

Every morning the owner gets an AI summary: cash collected, who's behind, attendance, what needs attention today.

Ask-anything assistant

"Who hasn't paid this month?" — answered from the club's real data, with 75+ actions it can take across every module.

AI onboarding

A new club gets set up by chatting with the platform — categories, schedules and fees configured in one conversation.
