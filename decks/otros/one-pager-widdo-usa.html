The Problem

  • 300K+ youth sports orgs in the US — 73% use spreadsheets & WhatsApp
  • Parents juggle 5+ apps and never know what's going on
  • Player data disappears when they switch clubs
  • No verified stats for college recruiting — NCSA charges families $4K/yr
  • US camps cost $1,500–$5,000. LATAM families lack credit cards. No flexible payment options

Widdo Solves It

  • One platform for clubs, tournaments, payments & families
  • Player profile is permanent and portable across clubs
  • Multi-role: be owner, coach, player & parent in different clubs
  • Verified Stats with Transparency Score — free for athletes
  • Built-in BNPL — Klarna/Affirm in US; Addi, Kueski, Pagaleve in LATAM via dLocal. Camp gets paid in full. Zero risk for Widdo
  • Multi-language (EN/ES, Portuguese ready) — built for global markets
  • AI assistant for coaches, owners & parents — nobody else has this
★

Player-Centric, Not Club-Centric

Unlike TeamSnap or SportsEngine, the player is the center — not the club. A player's verified stats, history, and College-Ready Profile stay in Widdo forever. If they switch clubs, their data follows them. Think LinkedIn for athletes.

HOW BNPL WORKS — Example: Orlando Camp $2,900
🇺🇸 USA: Klarna/Affirm/Afterpay via Stripe. Family splits $2,900 into installments. Camp receives $2,900 in full.
🌎 LATAM: 80% of families have no credit card. Addi (CO), Kueski (MX), Pagaleve (BR) approve by national ID in 30 sec. Family pays 6 installments in local currency.
🔗 dLocal (cross-border): Converts local currency → USD via one API (17 countries). Deposits directly to camp’s Stripe Connect account. Camp owner receives $2,900 USD in full.
Widdo earns ~4% application fee via Stripe Connect. Zero credit risk — BNPL providers assume all risk. Year 1: ~$120K additional revenue.
