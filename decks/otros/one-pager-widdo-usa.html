Widdo is the AI-native operating system for sports organizations — any sport, any age. One platform for payments, rosters, attendance, families, tournaments and communication, with AI doing the busywork. Built by two basketball lifers. Live with clubs in Latin America; U.S. launch in Florida, January 2027, U.S. pilots being selected now.

The problem we solve

Most sports organizations still run on forms, spreadsheets, group chats and chasing payments by hand — and the people running them are coaches, not administrators. Widdo replaces that stack with one platform, and the AI does the chasing, the reporting and the setup, so the owner can coach.
