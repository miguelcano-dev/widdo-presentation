# Widdo LinkedIn Strategy

## Page Info
- **Page:** linkedin.com/company/widdo
- **Tagline:** "The operating system for youth sports clubs"
- **Industry:** Software Development
- **Location:** Orlando, FL
- **Banner:** fuente `decks/assets/linkedin-banner.html` — generar el PNG con `node decks/assets/generate-banner.js` (el .png no está versionado)

## Rules
- All posts in English (target: USA investors, club owners, sports tech people)
- Always include an image — posts without images get 50% less reach
- Never share internal metrics (club count, users, MRR, churn, CAC)
- Never reveal growth strategy (tournament flywheel, acquisition playbook)
- Never mention Colombia, LATAM, or Portuguese — USA-only positioning
- Max 3-5 hashtags: #sportstech #youthsports #startup #saas #clubmanagement
- No "we're excited to announce..." — nobody cares
- No asking for likes or shares — end with a strong statement or question
- Post 2-3x/week from company page, 1x/week from personal profiles

## Content Pillars (4 pillars)

| Pillar | % | Goal |
|--------|---|------|
| Product — screenshots, features, before/after | 30% | Show it exists and works |
| Industry — data, problems, hot takes | 25% | Position as experts |
| Differentiators — player profiles, AI, tournaments | 25% | Stand out from competitors |
| Behind the scenes — founder journey, decisions | 20% | Human connection |

## Image Types

| Type | When | Source |
|------|------|--------|
| Screenshots | Feature posts | `decks/assets/` — add device mockup |
| Text cards | Data/hot take posts | Generate as PNG (dark theme) |
| Carousels (PDF) | Educational posts | Multi-slide, 1080x1080 |
| Real photos | Events, behind the scenes | Take them |

## Distribution Strategy
- Personal profiles (Miguel + Alwin) = 10x more reach than company page
- Every personal post should tag @Widdo
- Comment on youth sports / sports tech posts with substance (not "great post!")
- Join groups: Youth Sports Professionals, Sports Tech, SaaS Founders
- Invite connections to follow page (200/month free)
- Best posting time: Tuesday-Thursday, 8-10 AM EST

## Realistic Growth
- Month 1: 50-100 followers (organic, personal network)
- Month 3: 200-500 (consistent 2-3x/week posting)
- Month 6: 1,000+ (if content connects with audience)
- Quality > quantity: 200 club owners and investors > 5,000 random
