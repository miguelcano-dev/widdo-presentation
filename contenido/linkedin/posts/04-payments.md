# Post 04 — Payments
**Channel:** Widdo company page
**Pillar:** Product
**Image:** `decks/assets/gestion-pagos.png` in device mockup

---

Club directors used to chase parents for payments. Calling, texting, hoping someone remembers.

With Widdo, payments are automatic. Parents get invoices, pay online, and the club sees everything in real time.

Late fee reminders? Automatic. Receipts? Automatic. Partial payments? Supported.

Clubs should get paid on time. Parents should have clarity. That's it.
