Youth sports doesn't have a tech problem. It has an infrastructure problem.

The tech exists. Payments, scheduling, communication — all solved problems in every other industry.

But nobody has built the connective layer for sports clubs. The thing that ties players, families, coaches, payments, and schedules into one system that actually works together.

That's not a feature list. That's infrastructure. And infrastructure is what changes industries.
