When a player leaves a club, their data disappears. Stats, medical records, attendance history — gone.

Every other platform ties player data to the club. The club leaves, the data goes with it.

We built something different.

At Widdo, the player's profile is permanent. It follows them across clubs, across seasons, across sports. Their stats, documents, achievements — all in one place.

Players should own their data. Not the software.
