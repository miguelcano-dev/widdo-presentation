I spent 25 years building software for other companies. 4 months ago I started building my own.

Widdo is the operating system for youth sports clubs — players, payments, attendance, tournaments, all in one place.

No VC money. No team of 10. Just relentless execution.

I built it because my son plays basketball, and I saw firsthand how every club runs on chaos. Coaches texting parents for payments. Spreadsheets for rosters. Zero visibility for families.

If you work in sports, sports tech, or youth development — follow along.
