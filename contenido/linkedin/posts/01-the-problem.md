**Channel:** Widdo company page

73% of youth sports clubs in the US use zero paid software to manage their operations.

Registration lives in Google Forms. Payments in Venmo. Attendance in a coach's head. Communication in a WhatsApp group that nobody muted.

This isn't a tech problem. It's an infrastructure gap.

These clubs generate billions in revenue but operate like it's 2005. The tools exist for restaurants, salons, and gyms — but not for the 300,000+ sports organizations running youth athletics in America.

That's what we're building at Widdo.
