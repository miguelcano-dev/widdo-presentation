## 1. What Widdo is

Widdo is the **AI-native operating system for youth sports clubs**: dues collection,
rosters, attendance, schedules, parent communication, and tournaments in one platform,
with an AI assistant that does the work instead of just answering. Built in Orlando,
Florida. Two active products: **Widdo Clubs** (club operations) and **Widdo Tournaments**
(registrations, brackets, live scoring).

**Positioning already live on the page (write posts consistent with it):**

- Tagline: `The AI-native operating system for youth sports clubs — it chases the dues so you can coach.`
- Banner claim: `Run your club without chasing parents for money.` + pills `AI-native`,
  `0% platform fee`, `33 sports, basketball to cheer`.
- About: opens with the pain (volunteers, spreadsheets, group chats), then 3 audiences
  (owners / coaches / parents), then differentiators: 0% fee, AI-native, any sport/any age,
  clubs+tournaments in one, flat pricing by roster size.

**Content strategy rule (deliberate):** the About is outcomes-only, like ServiceTitan and
Brightwheel. **The specific product details live in FEED POSTS — that's your job.** Each
post takes ONE specific detail and makes it concrete.

## 3. Hard prohibitions

- ❌ Internal metrics: club count, users, MRR, churn, CAC, revenue. NEVER.
- ❌ Colombia, LATAM, Portuguese, Spanish-language posts. USA-only positioning. (Bilingual is a product FEATURE to talk about — in English.)
- ❌ Growth strategy: tournament flywheel, acquisition playbook, target lists.
- ❌ "We take 2-3% of payments" — false. Widdo takes 0%.
- ❌ Unvalidated numbers: "$12.5M recovered", "$71M debt", "21 clubs", "0% churn".
- ❌ "We're excited to announce…", begging for likes/shares, engagement bait.
- ❌ **Naming ANY competitor** — not TeamSnap, SportsEngine, LeagueApps, GotSport, Exposure,
  Crossbar, Jersey Watch, or anyone else. Say "legacy platforms", "the big registration
  platforms", "the old giants". Naming them gives them attention, SEO, and a reason to
  look at us. The review quotes are usable without brand attribution.
- ❌ Naming GameUp in any role (partnership in progress — don't touch it).
- ❌ Emoji as icons in designs. Draw real icons.

## 3b. Tournaments — second audience, first-class (internal note, never say this publicly)

Tournament posts are NOT a feature category — they target a **different reader: the
tournament director / event organizer**. Treat them as a first-class series (at least 1 in
4 posts), with their own pains: weekend chaos, paper brackets, teams asking for schedules,
parents asking for scores, collecting entry fees by Venmo/Zelle, no-shows at check-in.
Their CTA is the same (widdo.co / demo), their proof points: registrations, brackets in
6 formats, live scoring parents refresh from the stands, QR check-in, online entry payments
with 0% platform fee. **Never explain in public why tournaments matter strategically to
Widdo — no "flywheel", no "each tournament brings us clubs". Just serve the organizer.**

## 4. Voice

Direct, concrete, a little dry. Founder-built, not corporate. Short sentences. One idea
per post. End with a statement or a genuine question — never a CTA beg. 3-5 hashtags max
from: #sportstech #youthsports #clubmanagement #saas #startup. All posts in English.
Personal-profile versions (founder voice, "I") may be requested for each post — the
company page and Miguel's profile run the same story in different voice.

## 5. Design system for post images

- **Font:** Red Hat Display (Google Fonts), weights 400-900. "Widdo" always weight 800. Never Inter/Arial/Roboto.
- **Dark card (default):** background `#0A0A0F`, card `#12121A`, text `#E8E8ED`, secondary `rgba(232,232,237,0.5)`, accent green `#00C853`, subtle border `1px solid rgba(0,200,83,0.15)`, radius 12-16px.
- **Light variant:** background `#F6F8F6`, text `#0B1A12`, accent `#16A34A`.
- **Banner-family art** (optional): dark green-tinted `#0A1410` with faint court-line SVG circles — matches the page cover.
- **Sizes:** feed image 1200×627 or 1080×1080, export 2x. Carousel: PDF, 1080×1080, ≤10 slides.
- **Type scale on 1080×1080:** big stat 72-96px w900 · headline 36-48px w700-800 · body 20-24px w400-500.
- Product screenshots: source from `decks/assets/` (English UI only), framed in a device mockup.
- Hero pattern for stat cards: ONE huge number in green, one line of context below.
