## Descripción larga

```
Widdo is the AI-native operating system for youth sports clubs.

Most club software gives you screens. Widdo gives you an assistant that already
read your data: it finds who is overdue, drafts the reminders, flags the player
who has missed four of the last six sessions, and shows you a morning brief of
what changed. You approve; it executes. Nothing goes out under your club's name
without a person saying yes.

What it replaces:
- The spreadsheet only one person understands
- The group chat where the schedule change gets lost
- The clipboard nobody fills in after week three
- The Sunday night spent matching transfers to names

What clubs get:
- Payments: one balance per player, visible to the family. Autopay as the
  default at registration, not an option buried in settings. A board where
  green is paid, yellow is due soon and red is overdue — and a family that has
  already paid is never shown in red.
- Attendance: fifteen seconds on a phone, or a QR code at the door. The data
  then tells you which sessions are not working and which family is drifting.
- Registration: parents type their own details once, waivers are signed
  digitally against the right player, and the fee schedule attaches itself.
- Communication: announcements with read state, targeted by team, role or
  status. Every parent reads the app in English or Spanish, their choice.
- Tournaments: registrations, six bracket formats, live scoring, online entry
  payments and QR check-in that verifies who is cleared to play.

33 sports live today — basketball, baseball, volleyball, flag football, cheer,
lacrosse, swimming and more. Built for youth clubs first, and it runs adult
teams and leagues just as well.

Pricing is flat and per roster size, not per player: $99, $249 or $499 a month
for up to 80, 200 or 500 players. Every plan includes every module and the
assistant. Pay for twelve months and get thirteen.

Families' money lands straight in the club's own Stripe account. Widdo never
takes a cut of dues, registrations or tournament entries — the subscription is
the only thing you pay us.
```

puede escribir aquí.

  mayo-2026, y una reseña incentivada por el vendedor se retira.

  también en GetApp y Software Advice: tres reseñas, tres directorios.
- Pedirlas después de un momento bueno — el primer mes cobrado entero, el
