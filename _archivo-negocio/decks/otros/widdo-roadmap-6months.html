The Financial & Operational Infrastructure for Amateur Sports
🏆

Vision: Build the largest database of verified youth athletes in the Americas. Clubs operate on Widdo, tournaments drive growth, and families finance their path to USA sports opportunities through BNPL. The Toast of amateur sports.

Multi-Country Expansion

BUILDING
