Key Insights — What to Apply at Widdo

✅ LeagueApps Proves the Market

  • $20M revenue from 3,000 youth sports clients
  • ARPC: $550/month — Widdo targets $99-349
  • Backed by Arctos Sports Partners (sports-focused PE)
  • Apply: Use their metrics in pitch. "LeagueApps does $20M with just USA. We do USA + LATAM + BNPL"

⚠️ PlayVS: Don't Over-Raise

  • Raised $115M, now valued at $46M (0.40x)
  • Scaling before product-market fit destroyed value
  • Acquired competitors instead of building
  • Apply: $250K is the right amount. Prove PMF first, then scale capital

💡 Spond: Freemium Creates Volume

  • 3M+ monthly users with only $17M funding
  • Free for grassroots teams, monetize premium
  • No funding since 2021 — likely profitable/sustaining
  • Apply: Low/free pricing in LATAM to capture volume. Monetize via BNPL + referral commissions

🎯 LATAM = Zero Competition

  • 833 sports tech startups in LATAM (Tracxn), only 51 funded
  • ZERO doing club management + payments + cross-border
  • 61% of LATAM VC goes to fintech — Widdo fits perfectly
  • Apply: Position as "the first sports fintech for LATAM" to VC audiences
