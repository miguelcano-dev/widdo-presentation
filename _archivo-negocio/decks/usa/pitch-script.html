Problem & Solution
2
The Problem
1 min
300,000+ youth sports organizations in the US. 150 million Americans play organized sports. And 73% of community clubs use no paid software at all.

Clubs track players on spreadsheets. Collect payments through Venmo or cash. Send schedules on WhatsApp. Mark attendance on paper.

But the worst part? Player data disappears every time someone switches clubs. There's no portable profile. No verified stats. And if a family wants to pay $3,000 for a summer camp, there's no way to split it — especially in Latin America where 80% don't have credit cards.
For Erica specifically: pause on the "80% don't have credit cards" line. That's CardWorks DNA.
3
The Solution — Player-Centric
1 min
Widdo is player-centric, not club-centric. That's the key difference.

In TeamSnap or SportsEngine, if the club leaves the platform, the player loses everything. In Widdo, the player's profile lives forever — stats, history, documents, everything.

And it's multi-role: you can be a club owner, a coach, a player, AND a parent — in the same or different clubs. One account, every role.

Plus we have an AI assistant built in — coaches can ask "show me players with attendance below 70%" and get instant answers. Nobody else has this.

Multi-language — English and Spanish live, Portuguese ready. Built for global from day one.
If demoing on phone: open the app NOW and show a real club. "Let me show you instead of telling you."
4
The Ecosystem — 3 Verticals
45s
Widdo is not one product. It's three verticals that feed each other, all powered by a built-in payment and BNPL layer:

Widdo Clubs — live today. Complete club management. $99 to $349 per month. Families can split tuition payments with BNPL.

Widdo Tournaments — also live. AI-powered brackets, live scoring, verified stats. This is our viral engine. Registration and travel fees can be split with BNPL too.

Widdo Academy — coming later this year. Sports education marketplace. Course fees also splittable.

BNPL is not a separate product — it's the payment layer that powers everything. Klarna and Affirm in the US, local providers in LATAM. Every transaction in every vertical can be split. That's 3-5% fee on a $10.5 billion market.

The flywheel: clubs enter tournaments → tournaments bring new clubs → coaches discover Academy → take Widdo to their clubs. BNPL increases conversion at every step. Each piece feeds the others.
The Game Changers
5

1 min
This is what makes Widdo a $100 million company, not a $10 million SaaS.

One tournament brings 2,000+ new users in a single weekend.

Here's how: an organizer creates a tournament in Widdo. 32 teams register and pay through the platform. That's 800 players, 96 coaches, 1,200 parents — all creating accounts.

Live scoring, verified stats, public brackets. And when it's over, 5 to 10 of those clubs subscribe to Widdo for their own operations. They saw it in action. "How much for my club?"

There are 100,000+ youth tournaments in the US every year. Each one worth $15-50K. Widdo earns 5% of registrations plus 3% of gate admissions. We don't need ads — we need tournaments.
This is the "wow" slide. Let the "2,000 users in a weekend" number land. Pause after it.
6
The Payment Layer — BNPL Everywhere
1 min
Let me go deeper on the payment layer. Families can't pay $3,000 for a camp upfront. Whether it's a club tuition, a tournament fee, or a camp registration — BNPL is built into every transaction. In the US, we offer Klarna, Affirm, and Afterpay through Stripe.

In Latin America, it's even bigger. 80% of families don't have credit cards. We integrate local BNPL providers — Addi in Colombia, Kueski in Mexico, Pagaleve in Brazil — through dLocal. They approve by national ID in 30 seconds.

And here's the cross-border play: a grandmother in Medellín can pay for her grandson's camp in Orlando — in Colombian pesos. dLocal converts it, the camp owner gets dollars. Same platform.

62 million Hispanics in the US — we're the only bilingual platform. That's the bridge between both markets.

Widdo is NOT a lender. We just connect the pipes. Zero credit risk for us, 3-5% fee on every transaction. Near 100% margin.

It's the same concept your family built at CardWorks — serve the people traditional banks don't reach. But for sports families.
The CardWorks line is for Erica specifically. For other investors, replace with: "We're a fintech layer disguised as a sports platform."

It's the platform where every athlete's career lives — stats, payments, attendance, everything. Clubs pay us to manage their operations, but the player's profile is permanent. Think LinkedIn for sports.

"Are people paying for this?"
We launched as a free pilot to perfect the product with real users — 17 clubs, 1,000+ users, zero churn, all organic. Payments go live first week of April. We wanted the product right before we charged.

"Is this just in Colombia?"
We already have an academy in the US with 50 players, and 10+ clubs joining this week in Colombia. Strategy is USA first, then scale to LATAM and beyond from US credibility.

"It's just the two of you?"
Miguel built the entire platform — he's a 25-year senior dev who's worked for major US companies and startups worldwide. He also played pro basketball in Colombia. I handle business and growth. The code is fully documented and AI-assisted — we can onboard developers fast.

"How do you compete with TeamSnap / Crossbar?"
TeamSnap charges $400-600/month with hidden fees, ads, and no AI. Crossbar is closer to us — $995/year with tournaments — but they charge per-transaction fees on top, have no AI, no BNPL, no multi-language, and aren't player-centric. We charge $99/month flat, include an AI assistant, BNPL, tournaments, and the player's profile lives forever.

"What would you do with investment?"
First 100 US clubs, hire 2-3 developers, and build out the tournament viral loop. We hit breakeven in about 11 months. But honestly — I'd rather show you what we've built and get your thoughts first.

"How can I get involved?"
We're raising a small pre-seed via SAFE. I'll have Miguel send you the full deck and details — but your experience in sports infrastructure and payments is what excites us most.

"What about Cole / the Magic guys?"
Imagine if Cole had this when he was 14 — verified stats, his whole career tracked, coaches finding him through the platform instead of expensive showcases. That's what we're giving every kid.

"What's the valuation?"
We're raising on a SAFE with a cap. We're flexible on structure and focused on finding partners who understand the sports ecosystem. We can discuss specifics when there's mutual interest.

"What if TeamSnap copies you?"
TeamSnap is owned by private equity optimizing for ad revenue. They'd have to rebuild their entire architecture to become player-centric and add AI, BNPL, and tournaments. By then, we'll have the network effect. And they'll never be bilingual.
