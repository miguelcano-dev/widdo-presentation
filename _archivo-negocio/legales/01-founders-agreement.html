Transfer Restrictions & Stockholder Rights

11.1 Right of First Refusal (ROFR)

If Founder 2 wishes to sell, transfer, or otherwise dispose of any vested shares, the Company shall have the right of first refusal to purchase such shares on the same terms as the proposed transaction. If the Company declines, Founder 1 shall have the right of first refusal. Notice of the proposed sale must be given at least thirty (30) days prior to the intended transaction.

11.2 Drag-Along Rights

If stockholders holding more than fifty percent (50%) of the voting power of the Company approve a sale, merger, or other change of control transaction, all other stockholders shall be obligated to participate in such transaction on the same terms and conditions.

11.3 Tag-Along Rights

If Founder 1 proposes to sell more than fifty percent (50%) of his shares to a third party, Founder 2 shall have the right (but not the obligation) to sell a proportional number of his vested shares in the same transaction, on the same terms and conditions.

11.4 Change of Control

There shall be no automatic acceleration of vesting upon a Change of Control (defined as a sale, merger, or IPO). The Board may, at its discretion, negotiate double-trigger acceleration for Founder 2: acceleration occurs only if (1) a Change of Control occurs AND (2) Founder 2 is terminated without Cause within twelve (12) months of the Change of Control.
