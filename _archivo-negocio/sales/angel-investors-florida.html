Valuation Guide
How the SAFE cap works and how to negotiate with angels.

What is a "cap" in a SAFE?

When an angel gives you $250K with a SAFE @ $3M cap, you're saying: "Give me $250K now. When I raise a big round later (Series A), your $250K converts to shares as if the company was worth $3M — even if by then it's worth $10M, $20M or more." It's a guaranteed discount for investing early.

Dilution by Cap

Cap $250K buys... % of Company Recommendation
$3M $250K / $3M 8.3% Target — YC standard for pre-seed with built product
$2.5M $250K / $2.5M 10.0% Floor — if angel pushes back, this is still reasonable
$2M $250K / $2M 12.5% Absolute minimum — below this you're giving away the company
$1.5M $250K / $1.5M 16.7% WALK AWAY — predatory for a product this built
