02 — Multi-Role

6 Roles. One Platform.

Each person sees exactly what they need. Nothing more, nothing less.

Owner / Director

FULL ACCESS

Full dashboard: revenue, expenses, compliance, attendance, billing. 360° view of the entire club. Configure plans, teams, coaches, categories.

Administrator

OPERATIONS

Day-to-day operations: players, billing, documents, inventory. Similar access to owner but without financial configuration.

Only sees their teams. Takes attendance from their phone. Player profiles, session notes. No access to billing or finances.

Their profile, attendance, documents, practice and game calendar. Knows exactly when and where.

Parent / Family

Child's profile, attendance, pending payments, calendar, documents. Zero phone calls to the office.

The owner controls who sees what. No information leaks. No confusion.
