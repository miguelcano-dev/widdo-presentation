Florida Compliance

Are you tracking all of this?

Florida requires every youth sports organization to keep these documents current. Missing one = fines, lawsuits, or losing your license.

Requirement Who Frequency If Missing
Background Check Level 2 Every adult with minor contact July 1, 2026 Fines, lawsuits, loss of license
Sports Physical Every athlete Annual (365 days) Cannot participate
Concussion Protocol Every athlete Annual Cannot play same day
ECG Screening Grades 9-12 (FHSAA) July 1, 2026 Cannot try out or practice
SafeSport Certification Coaches (NGB-affiliated) Annual Cannot coach
Signed Waivers Every athlete At enrollment Club liable for injuries

Widdo tracks it automatically.

Every athlete and coach gets a traffic light status. Green = current. Yellow = expiring in 30 days. Red = expired. You get alerts before anything expires. When an auditor shows up — export everything in one click.

  • ✓ Configurable document types per club
  • ✓ Parents upload from their portal
  • ✓ Automatic alerts: 30 days + 7 days before expiration
  • ✓ PDF/Excel export for audits
  • ✓ Digital waivers — parents sign on their phone
