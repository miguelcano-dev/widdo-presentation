Coach
Credentials

Florida law, July 2026. Every coach. Background check. No exceptions.

The Problem

Florida HB 975 (effective July 1, 2026): Every adult with contact with minors in a youth sports organization must have a Level 2 Background Check (fingerprinting via FDLE).

Today: the director "trusts" the coach did it. No centralized tracking. Audit = nobody knows where the certificate is. The organization is liable, not the individual.

Widdo Solution

Dedicated credential tracking for every coach. Traffic light status. Coaches upload from their own portal. Director gets alerts.

Red status = this coach should NOT be on the field. Visual, immediate, undeniable.
