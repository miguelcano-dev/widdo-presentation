Purpose & Structure

This Agreement governs the service relationship between Widdo Inc and Widdo SAS, both controlled by the same individual (MIGUEL ANGEL CANO QUINONEZ, who owns 70% of Widdo Inc and 100% of Widdo SAS). As related parties under IRC Section 482 and Colombian ET Articles 260-1 to 260-11, the parties establish arm's length pricing for all intercompany transactions. Widdo SAS provides software development and operational services to Widdo Inc, and Widdo Inc licenses its intellectual property to Widdo SAS for local operations.

Pricing Methodology

4.1 Arm's Length Standard

All intercompany transactions shall be priced in accordance with the arm's length principle as required by:

  • United States: Internal Revenue Code Section 482
  • Colombia: Estatuto Tributario, Articles 260-1 through 260-11
  • International: OECD Transfer Pricing Guidelines for Multinational Enterprises

4.2 Selected Method: Cost Plus

The pricing for services provided by Widdo SAS to Widdo Inc shall be determined using the Cost Plus Method:

Service Fee = Total Direct and Indirect Costs + Markup of 15%

4.3 Cost Base

The cost base includes all direct and indirect costs incurred by Widdo SAS in providing the services, including but not limited to:

4.4 Markup Justification

The 15% markup is consistent with arm's length pricing for software development services in Latin America, as supported by:

  • Comparable market data for outsourced software development services (LATAM to USA)
  • OECD benchmarking studies for IT service providers
  • Industry standard for captive service centers in developing countries (10-20% range)
  • IRC Section 174 considerations: moderate markup reduces total tax drag on the group while maintaining compliance with both IRC §482 and Colombian ET Arts. 260-1 to 260-11

4.5 Annual Review

The pricing methodology and markup percentage shall be reviewed annually and adjusted if necessary to maintain arm's length compliance. Any adjustment shall be documented and justified.

Tax Compliance

9.1 United States

  • Form 5471: NOT required under current structure (SAS owned by Miguel personally, not by C Corp). Required only if SAS ownership is transferred to Widdo Inc in the future.
  • IRC Section 482: Applies to transactions between related parties controlled by the same person. IRS may adjust pricing if not at arm's length.
  • Form 1120: Intercompany service fees are deductible expenses for Widdo Inc

9.2 Colombia

  • Régimen Ordinario: Widdo SAS is taxed under the ordinary corporate income tax regime (35% on net profit). Service fee income from Widdo Inc is recognized as exportation of services under Art. 481 E.T. (IVA-exempt, zero-rated) provided the statutory requirements are met (written contract, services used exclusively abroad, supporting documentation retained)
  • IVA (VAT): Services provided to Widdo Inc are VAT-exempt as service exports. SaaS services in Colombia are excluded from IVA under Art. 476 Num. 21 E.T. (cloud computing services)
  • Transfer Pricing Declaration: Required if gross income exceeds ~61,000 UVT (~$3.8M USD) OR gross assets exceed ~37,000 UVT (~$2.3M USD). Below thresholds: maintain supporting documentation (functional analysis, comparability analysis) but formal declaration not required
  • Withholding Tax: Payments from the USA to Colombia for services are generally not subject to US withholding when the service is performed outside the USA and the Colombian entity has no US permanent establishment (verify specific rates and PE exposure with CPA)
  • RUB (Beneficiarios Finales): Miguel Angel Cano registered as ultimate beneficial owner of Widdo SAS (100%)

9.3 CFC Rules (Controlled Foreign Corporation)

Colombia's DIAN may classify Widdo Inc as a Controlled Foreign Corporation (CFC) under Estatuto Tributario Article 882-884 if a Colombian tax resident controls more than 10% of the entity. Mitigation: (a) Widdo Inc has real economic substance (USA clients, USA bank accounts, USA operations); (b) this risk is eliminated when Miguel Angel Cano becomes a US tax resident.
