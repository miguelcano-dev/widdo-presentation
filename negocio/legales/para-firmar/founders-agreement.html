Confidentiality

10.1 Each Founder agrees to maintain the confidentiality of all Confidential Information of the Company. "Confidential Information" includes but is not limited to: source code, algorithms, business plans, financial data, customer data, investor discussions, trade secrets, pricing strategies, and any information designated as confidential by the Company.

10.2 This obligation of confidentiality shall survive the termination of this Agreement and shall be perpetual.

10.3 Exceptions: Information that (a) is or becomes publicly available through no fault of the receiving Founder; (b) was known to the receiving Founder prior to the Company's formation; (c) is received from a third party without restriction; or (d) is required to be disclosed by law or legal process.

Transfer Restrictions & Stockholder Rights

11.1 Right of First Refusal (ROFR)

If either Founder wishes to sell, transfer, or otherwise dispose of any vested shares, the Company shall have the right of first refusal to purchase such shares on the same terms as the proposed transaction. If the Company declines, the other Founder shall have the right of first refusal. Notice of the proposed sale must be given at least thirty (30) days prior to the intended transaction.

11.2 Drag-Along Rights

If stockholders holding more than fifty percent (50%) of the outstanding shares approve a sale, merger, or other change of control transaction, all other stockholders shall be obligated to participate in such transaction on the same terms and conditions.

11.3 Tag-Along Rights

If Founder 1 proposes to sell more than fifty percent (50%) of his shares to a third party, Founder 2 shall have the right (but not the obligation) to sell a proportional number of his vested shares in the same transaction, on the same terms and conditions.

11.4 Change of Control

There shall be no automatic acceleration of vesting upon a Change of Control (defined as a sale, merger, or IPO). The Board may, at its discretion, negotiate double-trigger acceleration for Founder 2: acceleration occurs only if (1) a Change of Control occurs AND (2) Founder 2 is terminated without Cause within twelve (12) months of the Change of Control.

Non-Competition

12.1 During the term of this Agreement and for a period of twelve (12) months following separation, each Founder agrees not to directly or indirectly engage in, own, manage, operate, consult for, or be employed by any business that competes with the Company in the field of sports club management software, sports technology SaaS, or tournament management platforms.

12.2 This restriction applies worldwide.

12.3 Exception: Passive ownership of less than five percent (5%) of the shares of a publicly traded company shall not constitute a violation of this Section.

Non-Solicitation

13.1 For a period of twenty-four (24) months following separation, neither Founder shall directly or indirectly solicit, recruit, or hire any employee, contractor, client, investor, or business partner of the Company.

Cause for Removal

14.1 A Founder may be removed for "Cause," defined as any of the following:

  1. Fraud, embezzlement, misappropriation of Company assets, or criminal conduct
  2. Material breach of this Agreement that remains uncured for fifteen (15) days after written notice
  3. Directly or indirectly competing with the Company
  4. Abandonment of duties: more than thirty (30) consecutive days without meaningful activity and without prior written notice or justified reason
  5. Consistent failure to meet minimum time commitment as set forth in Section 6.3
  6. Gross negligence or willful misconduct in the performance of duties
  7. Conviction of a felony or crime involving moral turpitude

Separation & Termination

15.1 Voluntary Resignation: Either Founder may resign from their role with sixty (60) days written notice to the other Founder and the Board.

15.2 Effect of Separation: Upon separation of a Founder for any reason:

  1. Unvested shares are subject to repurchase by the Company at par value ($0.00001/share)
  2. Vested shares are retained by the departing Founder, subject to the Right of First Refusal
  3. Non-competition (12 months) and non-solicitation (24 months) obligations survive
  4. Confidentiality obligations survive perpetually
  5. All Company property (devices, accounts, credentials, materials) must be returned within seven (7) business days
  6. The departing Founder resigns from the Board of Directors effective upon separation

15.3 Founder 2 Cash Contribution: In the event of separation, the $15,000 USD contribution made by Founder 2 shall not be refundable, as it constitutes capital of the Company invested for shares and operational use.

Dispute Resolution

17.1 The Founders shall first attempt to resolve any dispute arising under this Agreement through good faith negotiation for a period of thirty (30) days.

17.2 If the dispute is not resolved through negotiation, it shall be submitted to binding arbitration administered by JAMS in Orlando, Florida, in accordance with JAMS Comprehensive Arbitration Rules.

17.3 The arbitration shall be conducted by a single arbitrator. The decision of the arbitrator shall be final and binding on both parties.

17.4 Each party shall bear their own legal costs and fees, unless the arbitrator determines that one party acted in bad faith, in which case the arbitrator may award reasonable attorneys' fees to the prevailing party.
