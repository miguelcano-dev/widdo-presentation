UNANIMOUS WRITTEN CONSENT
OF THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS

WIDDO INC. — a Delaware corporation
In Lieu of a Meeting

The undersigned, being all of the members of the Board of Directors (the "Board") of Widdo Inc., a Delaware corporation (the "Company"), acting by unanimous written consent in lieu of a meeting pursuant to Section 141(f) of the Delaware General Corporation Law and the Company's Bylaws, hereby adopt the following resolutions effective as of August 3, 2026.

1. Recitals

WHEREAS, between January and July 2026, prior to the Company establishing an operating bank account, Miguel Angel Cano Quinonez ("Founder 1") advanced personal funds to pay for infrastructure, software licenses, developer program fees and other operating expenses incurred by and for the benefit of the Company;

WHEREAS, such advances are evidenced by a Promissory Note dated August 3, 2026 (the "Note"), issued by the Company in favor of Founder 1, bearing interest at zero percent (0%) per annum, unsecured, and subordinate to any obligations of the Company to third-party investors, lenders or creditors;

WHEREAS, Section 3 of the Note provides that the principal shall be repaid at such time as the Company has sufficient funds available, as determined by the Board;

WHEREAS, the Company held USD $14,306.04 in its operating account at Mercury immediately prior to the payment, and has no outstanding obligations to third-party investors, lenders or creditors that would be subordinated by such repayment;

WHEREAS, the Board has reviewed the itemized schedule of advances and has determined that only those advances supported by third-party invoices or receipts on file shall be reimbursed, and has confirmed that each advance was charged to a personal payment instrument of Founder 1 (Mastercard ending 5072 or BBVA card ending 0968) and not to any account or card of the Company;

WHEREAS, on August 3, 2026 the Company transferred USD $1,476.46 from its Mercury operating account to Founder 1 in satisfaction of the Note, and the Board wishes to ratify, confirm and approve that payment in all respects;

The itemized detail of each advance is set forth in Schedule A to the Note and is incorporated herein by reference. This Consent does not restate that schedule in order to avoid divergence between the two documents.

3. Resolutions

Resolved — Sufficiency of Funds RESOLVED, that the Board hereby determines that the Company had, at the time of payment, sufficient funds available within the meaning of Section 3 of the Note to repay the principal amount set forth in Section 2 above.
Resolved — Repayment Ratified RESOLVED FURTHER, that the payment made on August 3, 2026 to Founder 1 of the aggregate principal amount of USD $1,476.46, in a single payment from the Company's Mercury operating account and in full and final satisfaction of the Note, is hereby ratified, confirmed and approved in all respects as the act of the Company.
Resolved — Discharge of Note RESOLVED FURTHER, that the Note shall be marked "PAID IN FULL", shall be of no further force or effect, and shall be retained in the Company's records; and that no further amounts are or shall be owed by the Company to Founder 1 in respect of advances made during the period covered by the Note.
Resolved — Accounting Treatment RESOLVED FURTHER, that such payment shall be recorded in the Company's books as a repayment of a shareholder loan (a reduction of the Company's liabilities), and not as compensation, salary, distribution, dividend, or operating expense; and that the payment reference shall read "Repayment of Founder Loan — Promissory Note."
Resolved — Reporting RESOLVED FURTHER, that the Officers are directed to ensure that both the original advances and this repayment are reported to the Company's tax advisors as transactions with a foreign related party, for purposes of any filing required under IRS Form 5472 and the Company's Form 1120.
Resolved — General Authority RESOLVED FURTHER, that the Officers of the Company are authorized to take any and all actions and execute any documents reasonably necessary to carry out the intent of the foregoing resolutions.

4. Interested Director Disclosure

Founder 1 is a director of the Company and the payee under the Note, and is therefore an interested party with respect to these resolutions. Founder 2 confirms that he has reviewed the Note and the supporting documentation, that the transaction is fair to the Company, and that he approves it with full knowledge of Founder 1's interest, in accordance with Section 144 of the Delaware General Corporation Law.

5. Execution

This Consent may be executed in counterparts and by electronic signature, each of which shall be deemed an original, and shall be filed with the minutes of the proceedings of the Board.
