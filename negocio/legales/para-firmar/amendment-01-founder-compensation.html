WHEREAS, the Board wishes to authorize a temporary, fixed-term stipend for Founder 1 that terminates automatically, rather than a salary of indefinite duration, and to do so with the full and informed consent of Founder 2;

3. Amendment to Section 7 of the Agreement

Sections 7.1 through 7.4 of the Agreement are hereby supplemented by the following provisions. Except as expressly modified below, Section 7 and all other provisions of the Agreement remain in full force and effect.

7.5 — Founder Transition Stipend Notwithstanding Sections 7.1 and 7.3, the Company shall pay Founder 1 a transition stipend of USD $2,000 per month for a maximum of three (3) monthly payments, covering August, September and October 2026, for an aggregate maximum of USD $6,000. The purpose of this stipend is to bridge Founder 1's personal income while the Company raises a financing round; it is not compensation for services at market rate and does not establish an employment relationship. Founder 2 shall receive no compensation and shall accrue no deferred compensation during this period, consistent with his part-time commitment under Section 6.3.
7.6 — Automatic Sunset and Early Termination This stipend terminates automatically upon the earliest to occur of:
  (a) October 31, 2026;
  (b) the occurrence of a triggering event under Section 7.1 (a Qualified Financing of at least USD $200,000, or the Company achieving USD $5,000 in Monthly Recurring Revenue), upon which the salary schedule set forth in Section 7.1 shall apply in lieu of this stipend; or
  (c) the first full calendar month in which Founder 1 receives recurring external income of USD $2,000 or more per month, which Founder 1 shall report to the Board in writing within ten (10) days of it commencing.

Termination occurs without any action by either party. This stipend shall not renew, extend, or convert into a salary, and no expectation of continued compensation arises from it. Any extension or reinstatement requires a new unanimous written consent of the Board adopted prior to any further payment. Permanent founder salaries remain subject to the triggering events in Section 7.1 as originally drafted.
7.7 — Cash Guardrail No payment under Section 7.5 shall be made if, after giving effect to such payment, the Company's operating account balance would fall below USD $4,000. A payment withheld under this provision is cancelled, not deferred, and creates no liability of the Company to Founder 1.
7.8 — Payment Structure and Tax Treatment Founder 1 shall be paid as an independent contractor rendering services from Colombia. This provision supersedes, for the duration of the stipend only, the requirement in Section 7.2 that Founder 1 be compensated through Widdo SAS. Founder 1 shall deliver a signed IRS Form W-8BEN to the Company before the first payment. The parties acknowledge that (a) the services are performed entirely outside the United States and the resulting income is accordingly treated as foreign-source income not subject to U.S. withholding, subject to confirmation by the Company's tax advisors; (b) Founder 1 is solely responsible for his own tax obligations in Colombia; and (c) these payments are reportable transactions with a foreign related party for purposes of IRS Form 5472.
7.9 — Temporary Reduction of Time Commitment The Board acknowledges that Founder 1 may need to accept supplementary paid work while the Company is unable to fund a market-rate salary. Accordingly, and notwithstanding Section 6.3, a reduction of Founder 1's dedication to no fewer than twenty-five (25) hours per week during the period from August 1, 2026 through [FECHA FIN — sugerido: March 31, 2027] shall not constitute a breach of the Agreement and shall not trigger the remedies in Section 14, provided that Founder 1 remains CEO, continues to direct product and engineering, and reports any such external engagement to the Board in writing. Founder 1 shall not accept work for any competitor of the Company as defined in Section 13.
7.10 — Informed Consent of Founder 2 Regarding Use of Capital Founder 2 expressly acknowledges and agrees that the capital contribution of USD $15,000 made by him under Section 4.2 constitutes capital of the Company available for general operational use, that the payments authorized in Section 7.5 (up to USD $6,000, being approximately 40% of that contribution) will be funded from the Company's operating account and therefore in substance from such capital, and that he approves this use with full knowledge of the foregoing. Founder 2 further confirms that this Amendment does not alter his equity position, his vesting schedule, the milestones set forth in Section 5.3, or the non-refundable character of his contribution.

4. Resolutions

Resolved — Amendment Approved RESOLVED, that Amendment No. 1 to the Founders Agreement, in the form set forth in Section 3 above, is hereby approved and adopted in all respects.
Resolved — Payments Authorized RESOLVED FURTHER, that the Officers of the Company are authorized and directed to pay Founder 1 up to three (3) monthly payments of USD $2,000 each, aggregating no more than USD $6,000, from the Company's Mercury operating account, subject in each case to the cash guardrail in Section 7.7, with each payment referenced "Founder Transition Stipend — Amendment No. 1."
Resolved — Accounting and Reporting RESOLVED FURTHER, that such payments shall be recorded as contractor compensation expense of the Company — and expressly not as a repayment of the Founder Loan, a distribution, or a dividend — and that the Officers shall report them to the Company's tax advisors as transactions with a foreign related party for purposes of IRS Form 5472 and the Company's Form 1120.
Resolved — General Authority RESOLVED FURTHER, that the Officers of the Company are authorized to take any and all actions and execute any documents reasonably necessary to carry out the intent of the foregoing resolutions, including the execution of an independent contractor services agreement with Founder 1 consistent with Section 7.8.

5. Interested Director Disclosure

Founder 1 is a director of the Company and the sole payee under this Amendment, and is therefore an interested party with respect to these resolutions. Founder 2 confirms that he has reviewed this Amendment in full, including the disclosure in Section 7.10 regarding the source of the funds, that he considers the arrangement fair to the Company, and that he approves it with full knowledge of Founder 1's interest, in accordance with Section 144 of the Delaware General Corporation Law.
