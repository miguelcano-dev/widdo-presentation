Terms

1. Principal. FOR VALUE RECEIVED, the Borrower (Widdo Inc) promises to pay to the Lender (Miguel Angel Cano Quinonez) the aggregate principal sum set forth in Schedule A attached hereto, representing legitimate operational expenses advanced by the Lender on behalf of the Borrower.

2. Interest Rate. This Note shall bear interest at a rate of zero percent (0%) per annum. No interest shall accrue on the outstanding principal balance.

3. Repayment. The principal shall be repaid at such time as the Borrower has sufficient funds available, as determined by the Borrower's Board of Directors, in one or more payments. There is no fixed maturity date. The Borrower may prepay this Note in whole or in part at any time without penalty.

4. Subordination. This Note is subordinate to any obligations of the Borrower to third-party investors, lenders, or creditors. Repayment of this Note shall not take priority over the Company's operational needs, payroll obligations, or investor obligations.

5. Purpose. The funds advanced by the Lender were used exclusively for legitimate business expenses of the Borrower, including but not limited to: cloud infrastructure (DigitalOcean), artificial intelligence development tools (Anthropic Claude), transactional email services (Resend), domain registration, and developer program licenses (Apple, Google) necessary for the development and operation of the Company's software platform.

6. No Security. This Note is unsecured. The Lender shall have no lien or security interest in any assets of the Borrower.

7. Documentation. The Lender shall maintain a detailed record of all expenses advanced (the "Founder Loan Tracker"), including dates, amounts, descriptions, and supporting receipts. This record shall be made available to the Borrower's Board of Directors and CPA upon request.

8. Tax Treatment. Both parties acknowledge that: (a) this Note represents a bona fide loan and not compensation, equity, or a capital contribution; (b) repayment of principal is not taxable income to the Lender; (c) the zero-interest rate is acceptable for loans under $10,000 USD per IRS guidelines; and (d) if the outstanding balance exceeds $10,000, the parties will consult with a CPA regarding IRS Applicable Federal Rate requirements.

9. Assignment. This Note may not be assigned by either party without the written consent of the other party.

10. Governing Law. This Note shall be governed by the laws of the State of Delaware.
