Information We Collect

When you create an account: full name, email address, phone number, password (stored hashed), profile photo (optional), role within your club, language preference.

1.2 Club Information

When a club is registered: club name, logo, description, address, sport types, subscription plan, billing information, payment gateway credentials (encrypted at rest).

1.3 Athlete & Member Information

Full name, date of birth, gender, position/role, photo (optional), emergency contacts, medical notes (optional, entered by the club), attendance records, performance statistics, parent/guardian information for minors.

Login timestamps, IP addresses, pages visited, features used, device type, browser, operating system, error logs.

1.6 AI Interactions

Conversations with the AI assistant, including messages sent and responses received.

1.7 Communications

Notifications, announcements, and messages sent through the platform.

Data Security

We implement the following security measures to protect your data:

  • Encryption in transit: All data is transmitted over HTTPS/TLS
  • Encryption at rest: Database and file storage are encrypted
  • Password hashing: bcrypt algorithm (industry standard)
  • Access control: Role-based permissions with 6 levels
  • Multi-tenant isolation: Row-level security + application-level checks
  • Authentication: Token-based (Laravel Sanctum) with refresh token rotation
  • Rate limiting: Protection against brute-force attacks
  • Input validation: XSS, CSRF, and SQL injection prevention
  • Backups: Automatic daily backups with 7-day point-in-time recovery
