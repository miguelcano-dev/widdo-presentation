Events

1.1 Equity Financing

If there is an Equity Financing before the termination of this SAFE, on the initial closing of such Equity Financing, this SAFE will automatically convert into the number of shares of Safe Preferred Stock equal to the Purchase Amount divided by the Safe Price.

The "Safe Price" means the price per share equal to the Post-Money Valuation Cap divided by the Company Capitalization.

In the event the Safe Price is higher than the price per share of the Standard Preferred Stock sold in the Equity Financing, then the Safe Price shall be the price per share of the Standard Preferred Stock (i.e., the investor gets the better of the two prices).

1.2 Liquidity Event

If there is a Liquidity Event (acquisition, IPO, or similar transaction) before the termination of this SAFE, the Investor will, at their option, receive either:

  1. A cash payment equal to the Purchase Amount; or
  2. A number of shares of Common Stock equal to the Purchase Amount divided by the Liquidity Price (defined as the Valuation Cap divided by the Company Capitalization, or the fair market value per share, whichever is lower).

1.3 Dissolution Event

If there is a Dissolution Event before the termination of this SAFE, the Company will pay the Investor an amount equal to the Purchase Amount, with priority over holders of Common Stock but subordinate to creditors of the Company.

Definitions

"Company Capitalization" means the sum of: (a) all shares of capital stock outstanding; (b) all shares underlying outstanding options, warrants, and convertible instruments (including SAFEs); and (c) shares reserved for future issuance under equity incentive plans. This is calculated on a post-money basis (i.e., includes the shares to be issued upon conversion of all SAFEs).

"Equity Financing" means a bona fide transaction in which the Company issues and sells Preferred Stock at a fixed valuation, with aggregate gross proceeds to the Company of not less than $250,000 USD (excluding conversion of SAFEs or other convertible instruments).

"Liquidity Event" means a Change of Control or an Initial Public Offering.

"Post-Money Valuation Cap" means $2,000,000 USD.

"Purchase Amount" means $[AMOUNT] USD.

"Safe Preferred Stock" means a series of Preferred Stock issued to the Investor with identical rights to the Standard Preferred Stock, except that the per-share liquidation preference and conversion price will be based on the Safe Price.

The Investor represents and warrants to the Company that:

  2. The Investor is purchasing this SAFE for their own account, for investment purposes only, and not with a view to distribution or resale.
  3. The Investor understands that the purchase involves substantial risks, including the possibility of losing the entire investment.
  4. The Investor has had the opportunity to ask questions and receive information from the Company regarding the business, operations, and financial condition.

Pro-Rata Right

The Investor shall have the right, but not the obligation, to purchase their pro rata share of the Standard Preferred Stock sold in the Equity Financing. The Investor's pro rata share shall be calculated based on the ratio of (a) the number of shares of capital stock held by the Investor (on an as-converted basis) immediately prior to the Equity Financing to (b) the total number of shares of capital stock outstanding (on a fully diluted basis) immediately prior to the Equity Financing.

Most Favored Nation (MFN)

If the Company issues any subsequent SAFEs prior to the initial closing of an Equity Financing with terms more favorable to the investor (including a lower valuation cap or addition of a discount), this SAFE shall automatically be amended to incorporate such more favorable terms, at the option of the Investor.
