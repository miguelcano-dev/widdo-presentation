## 1. DEFINITIONS

For the purposes of this Agreement, the following terms shall have the meanings set forth below:

**"Arm's Length Principle"** means the internationally accepted standard that prices charged between related parties should be equivalent to those that would be charged between unrelated parties in comparable transactions under comparable circumstances, as defined in Section 482 IRC, OECD Transfer Pricing Guidelines, and Colombian tax law.

**"Cost Base"** means the total documented and verifiable costs incurred by Widdo SAS in providing the Services to Widdo Inc., calculated on a monthly basis, as further described in Section 4.

**"Services"** means the software development, consulting, and related technology services described in Section 2 and any Statement of Work ("**SOW**") executed hereunder.

**"Related Party"** means a party that, directly or indirectly, controls, is controlled by, or is under common control with another party, as defined by Colombian Decree 2120 of 2017 and IRC Section 482.

## 4. COMPENSATION AND PAYMENT TERMS

### 4.1 Pricing Methodology — Cost Plus 15%

The Parties have determined, based on a functional analysis of the Services provided and a benchmarking study of comparable uncontrolled transactions (CUT) for software development services between unrelated parties in similar circumstances, that an appropriate arm's length compensation for the Services is a **markup of fifteen percent (15%)** over the Cost Base incurred by Widdo SAS.

The Parties acknowledge that this methodology is consistent with:

- Section 482 IRC and Treasury Regulations thereunder (specifically the **Services Cost Method** and **Comparable Profits Method**);
- OECD Transfer Pricing Guidelines, Chapter VII (Intra-group services);
- Colombian Decree 2120 of 2017 and Article 260 of the Estatuto Tributario;
- Industry benchmarks for offshore software development services (typical arm's length markups range from 5% to 20% for similar services).

### 4.2 Cost Base Components

The Cost Base shall include, for each calendar month, the following documented and verifiable costs incurred by Widdo SAS in providing the Services:

(a) **Personnel costs**, including salaries, wages, mandatory social security contributions (EPS, AFP, ARL, parafiscales), payroll taxes, non-salary benefits (auxilios), bonuses, and other compensation-related expenses of personnel engaged in providing the Services;

(b) **Infrastructure and technology costs**, including cloud services (AWS, Google Cloud, Microsoft Azure), third-party software licenses, AI services (Claude, OpenAI), domain registration, email services, development tools, and related technology expenses;

(c) **Professional services costs**, including legal fees, accounting fees, and other professional fees directly related to the performance of the Services;

(d) **Office and administrative costs**, including rent (if applicable), utilities, communications, office supplies, and other general and administrative expenses reasonably allocable to the Services;

(e) **Depreciation and amortization** of tangible and intangible assets used in providing the Services;

(f) **Travel and training costs** reasonably incurred in connection with the Services.

The Cost Base **shall not include** any expenses unrelated to the Services, extraordinary items, or non-recurring items not reasonably associated with the ordinary course of business.

### 4.3 Monthly Fee Calculation

The monthly Service Fee shall be calculated as follows:

```
Service Fee = Cost Base × 1.15
```

### 4.4 Invoicing

Widdo SAS shall issue to Widdo Inc. a monthly **electronic invoice** ("**factura electrónica**") in accordance with Colombian invoicing regulations, denominated in **United States Dollars (USD)**, within the first ten (10) calendar days of each month for Services rendered during the immediately preceding month.

Each invoice shall include:

(a) Description of Services provided;
(b) Cost Base breakdown by category;
(c) Applied markup (15%);
(d) Total Service Fee in USD;
(e) Reference to the applicable exchange rate (TRM published by the Colombian Central Bank, *Banco de la República*);
(f) A statement that the Services constitute an **export of services** pursuant to Article 481 of the Colombian Tax Code, and are therefore exempt from Colombian VAT (IVA).

### 4.5 Payment

Widdo Inc. shall pay each undisputed invoice within **thirty (30) days** of receipt, via wire transfer in USD to Widdo SAS's designated compensation account (*cuenta de compensación*) registered with the Central Bank of Colombia (*Banco de la República*).

Payments shall be made in United States Dollars. Any currency conversion into Colombian Pesos (COP) shall be made by Widdo SAS upon receipt, at its sole discretion, at prevailing market exchange rates.

### 4.6 Annual Review and True-Up

The Parties shall review the pricing methodology **annually**, no later than March 31 of each calendar year, to ensure continued compliance with the arm's length principle. If a review reveals that the actual financial results materially deviate from the target markup of 15%, the Parties may execute a **true-up adjustment** consisting of an additional invoice or credit note.

### 4.7 Documentation Requirements

Widdo SAS shall maintain comprehensive documentation supporting the Cost Base calculation, including:

(a) General ledger and subsidiary ledgers;
(b) Payroll records;
(c) Vendor invoices and expense reports;
(d) Time tracking records (where applicable);
(e) Bank statements reflecting payments received.

Widdo Inc. shall maintain corresponding documentation regarding receipt and utilization of the Services. Both Parties shall cooperate to maintain **contemporaneous documentation** sufficient to support the arm's length nature of the pricing in the event of an audit by the Colombian DIAN or the United States IRS.

## DRAFTING NOTES (Not part of the Agreement — for internal review only)

### For Colombian accountant (contadora SAS):

1. **IVA treatment:** Services qualify as export under Art. 481 ET → exempt. Must be declared in bimonthly VAT return (Form 300) as exports. Maintain supporting documentation.

2. **Income tax:** SAS operates under Régimen Ordinario. Revenue from this Agreement is taxable at 35% on net profit (after deducting cost base). The 15% markup represents the taxable profit margin.

3. **Transfer pricing reporting:** Review annually whether SAS's gross patrimony exceeds 100,000 UVT (~$5B COP) or whether operations with related parties exceed 61,000 UVT (~$3B COP) under Decree 2120/2017. If yes, formal transfer pricing study required. For Year 1, likely not applicable.

4. **Electronic invoicing:** Issue facturas electrónicas in USD with TRM reference. The DIAN's electronic invoicing system (Facturador Gratuito or a certified provider) supports foreign currency invoicing.

5. **Withholding taxes:** No withholding tax applies when SAS invoices to a foreign related party. However, verify whether Widdo Inc. withholds any U.S. taxes before remitting payment.

6. **Compensation account:** SAS should register a compensation account (cuenta de compensación) with the Central Bank of Colombia before receiving the first wire, to maintain USD and avoid automatic conversion at unfavorable rates.

### For US CPA (Widdo Inc.):

1. **Form 5472:** MANDATORY. Filed annually with Form 1120. Penalty for failure: $25,000 USD per year. Include all transactions with Widdo SAS.

2. **Section 174 classification:** The services received from Widdo SAS constitute **foreign research and development** for purposes of IRC Section 174. Must be capitalized and amortized over **15 years** (not 5 years as domestic R&D). This creates "phantom income" in early years — discuss mitigation strategies.

3. **Section 482 transfer pricing:** The 15% markup should be supported by a benchmarking study (CUT or Comparable Profits Method). Maintain contemporaneous documentation to avoid Section 6662 penalties.

4. **R&D Tax Credit (Section 41):** Portion of payments to Widdo SAS may qualify as qualified research expenses (QREs) if the services meet the four-part test. Consult with R&D tax credit specialist.

5. **FDII (Foreign-Derived Intangible Income):** If Widdo Inc. exports software/services to foreign customers, a portion of income may qualify for the preferential 13.125% tax rate under Section 250 IRC. Verify eligibility annually.

6. **Treaty benefits:** Check whether any provisions of the U.S.-Colombia Tax Treaty (currently limited — no comprehensive treaty) or Tax Information Exchange Agreement apply.
