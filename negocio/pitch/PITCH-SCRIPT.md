# Pitch Script — Widdo

Guion para presentar Widdo ante inversores. Total: **3-5 minutos**.

---

## Elevator Pitch (30 segundos)

> "150 million Americans play organized sports, but every club still runs on WhatsApp, spreadsheets, and paper forms. Widdo is the platform where clubs manage players, payments, tournaments, and communication — and every athlete builds a sports resume that follows them for life. We're live with 9 clubs — 3 paying annual subscriptions up front — and entering the US market, a $3.7 billion opportunity."

## Elevator Pitch (10 segundos)

> "Widdo — every club, every tournament, every athlete. One platform for the entire sports journey, from academy to arena."

---

## Pitch Completo (slide por slide)

### Slide 1: Cover (10 segundos)

> "Hi, I'm Miguel Cano, founder of Widdo. Every club. Every tournament. Every athlete. We're building the platform for the entire sports journey — from academy to arena."

**Tip:** Sonreir. Contacto visual. No leer el slide.

---

### Slide 2: The Problem (30 segundos)

> "150 million Americans play organized sports. But here's the thing — every single club still runs on spreadsheets, WhatsApp groups, and paper forms.
>
> Directors spend 15-20 hours a week on admin instead of coaching. Payments get lost. Attendance is tracked on paper. There's no single system that connects everything.
>
> It's a $40 billion industry running on tools from the '90s."

**Tip:** Dejar que el numero "150 million" aterrice. Pausa dramatica.

---

### Slide 3: Sports Landscape (20 segundos)

> "This isn't just one sport. Basketball alone has 30 million players. Soccer 14 million. Football, volleyball, swimming — 20 out of 24 team sports are growing year over year. And it's not just kids — adult recreational leagues are the fastest growing segment."

**Tip:** No leer la tabla. Highlight 2-3 datos clave.

---

### Slide 4: Multi-Sport Spotlight (15 segundos)

> "We built Widdo to work for ANY club — from a 50-kid basketball academy in Miami to a 500-member adult soccer league in Texas. Same platform, different needs, all covered."

**Tip:** Rapido, transicion hacia la solucion.

---

### Slide 5-6: Solution / How it Works (45 segundos)

> "Widdo gives every club a complete platform from day one. The director gets a dashboard with KPIs, billing, and alerts. Coaches manage sessions and track attendance with one tap. Parents see their kid's schedule, pay fees, and get notifications — all in a single app.
>
> No integrations needed. No setup fees. They sign up and they're running in 30 minutes."

**Tip:** Si puedes, mostrar el producto en VIVO aqui. Nada vende mas que un demo real.

---

### Slide 7: Market Size (30 segundos)

> "The global sports management software market is $10.2 billion, growing 11% per year. North America alone is $3.7 billion.
>
> Our target: 15,000 clubs in the next three to five years. At a blended $184 a month across our three tiers, that's $33.1 million in annual recurring revenue — less than one percent of the North American market."

**Tip:** Enfatizar que el SOM es conservador. Los inversores odian proyecciones infladas.

---

### Slide 8-9: Competition (20 segundos)

> "Yes, there are players — TeamSnap, SportsEngine, LeagueApps. But they all have the same problem: they're expensive, fragmented, and none of them serve the full club lifecycle.
>
> TeamSnap starts at $10 but organization plans cost $80 to $500 a month. And none of them speak Spanish or serve the Hispanic market — 62 million people in the US alone."

**Tip:** No atacar competidores. Posicionar la diferencia.

---

### Slide 10-11: Traction & Why Widdo (30 segundos)

> "We're not pitching an idea. We have a live product. 9 clubs live, 3 paying today in Latin America — and they paid a full year up front. 1,476 users on the platform. The platform is fully built — director dashboard, coach tools, parent app, payments, attendance, everything.
>
> What makes us different: we're multi-sport, multi-language, all-in-one, and we cost a fraction of the competition. And we have a massive unfair advantage in the Hispanic market."

**Tip:** El prepago anual es tu dato mas poderoso: dinero en el banco antes de pedirlo. NUNCA usar "zero churn" ni "0% churn" — con 3 clubes pagando no es un dato defendible y te quema la credibilidad para el resto de la reunion.

---

### Slide 12: Business Model (20 segundos)

> "Simple SaaS model. Three tiers: Starter at $99, Pro at $249, Club+ at $499 per month. We take zero percent of the money families pay — it goes straight to the club's own Stripe account. That's deliberate: taking a cut of every registration is the loudest complaint against LeagueApps and SportsEngine, so it's our way in."

**Tip:** NUNCA digas que Widdo se queda un 2-3% de los pagos. widdo.co promete "0% platform fee" en el hero y el codigo no cobra comision; decir lo contrario en una reunion te deja en contradiccion con tu propia web. La suscripcion es la unica fuente de ingreso hoy.

---

### Slide 13: Unit Economics (30 segundos)

> "Our unit economics are exceptional for early stage. $34 average revenue per club. $4 cost to serve. That's a 88% gross margin.
>
> Customer acquisition cost today is zero — it's all organic. Even projecting $50 CAC with marketing, our LTV-to-CAC ratio is 16x. We break even at just 60 clubs."

**Tip:** Este slide gana o pierde la reunion. Los numeros hablan solos.

---

### Slide 14-15: Product (15 segundos)

> "Here's the product live. Director dashboard, player management, payments, calendar — everything a club needs."

**Tip:** Si hiciste demo en vivo, pasar rapido estos slides.

---

### Slide 16: Roadmap & Ecosystem (20 segundos)

> "Widdo is not just club management — it's an ecosystem with three verticals.
>
> Widdo Clubs for operations. Widdo Tournaments for competition — brackets, live results, payments. Each tournament brings new clubs into the ecosystem. And Widdo Academy — think Platzi or Udemy for the sports world. Experts teach coaching, sports nutrition, club management, and monetize their knowledge.
>
> All three connect. A club operates with Clubs, competes with Tournaments, and their staff trains on Academy. Network effect: the more people join, the more valuable it becomes for everyone. And each vertical generates independent revenue."

---

### Slide 17: Go-to-Market (20 segundos)

> "We start in Florida — largest Hispanic population, year-round sports. Then Texas and California. Our playbook: land one club in a league, deliver results, and the whole league adopts. We've seen this happen organically in Colombia."

---

### Slide 18-19: Team (15 segundos)

> "I'm Miguel Cano — I built the entire platform solo in 6 months. Full-stack, backend, frontend, mobile, infrastructure. I'm not just the idea person; I'm the person who builds.
>
> Our founding partners in Orlando bring the US market: direct access to NBA and MLB investors, connections to Orlando Magic ownership, and boots on the ground in Florida. I build the product, they open the doors."

**Tip:** Mostrar que hay equipo complementario. Tech founder + BD partners en USA = combinacion fuerte.

---

### Slide 20: The Ask (30 segundos)

> "We're raising $350K in pre-seed on a YC standard post-money SAFE with a $3 million cap. We're taking a first close from $250K, so nobody waits on anybody. MFN, no discount, pro-rata for checks of $50K and up.
>
> That's eighteen months of runway. Here's where it goes:
>
> 1. My salary — $115K, $6,400 a month. That replaces the outside income I live on today, so I'm 100% on Widdo, and it's reviewed at month 12 against $5K MRR
> 2. A dedicated sales hire in Florida from month 6 — $65K on target. Until then, sales are founder-led plus referrals on commission
> 3. Marketing and acquisition in Florida — $32K: paid ads, English and Spanish content, and local leagues and events
> 4. Alwin part-time on business development in Orlando — $18K, still to be finalized
> 5. Legal, accounting and infrastructure — $25K
> 6. Reserve — $95K, which is six extra months of burn or pulling the sales hire forward
>
> Average burn is $14,200 a month with the rep on board, $10,600 without. At 100 clubs we're at $5K MRR and ready for a seed round."

**Tip:** Ser especifico sobre el uso del dinero. Inversores odian "lo vamos a usar para crecer".

**Sobre el salario:** decirlo sin rodeos y sin pedir disculpas. Es un tercio de la ronda y es la
partida mas grande, asi que el inversor la va a ver. La frase es "it replaces the outside income
I live on today, so I'm 100% on Widdo" — el inversor esta comprando 18 meses de atencion completa
del fundador. Nunca decir "me pago poco" en tono de disculpa: $6.400/mes es un salario de CEO por
debajo de mercado en USA para un fundador tecnico que construyo todo el producto.

**Sobre el cap:** es un **cap post-money** — el techo de valoracion al que el SAFE convierte en
la siguiente ronda de equity, y sobre el que se calcula la dilucion. $350K sobre un cap de $3M
= 11,7% para los inversores. Es el mismo cap que vio nuestra primera inversora en marzo; no se
bajo. Si preguntan por pre-money, corregir: "it's a post-money cap, not a pre-money valuation".

---

### Slide 21: Closing (10 segundos)

> "300,000 sports organizations in the US. Zero unified platform. We're building it. Thank you."

**Tip:** Silencio. Sonrisa. Esperar preguntas.

---

## Reglas de Oro del Pitch

1. **Nunca leer los slides** — Tu sabes la historia mejor que nadie
2. **Contar una historia** — Empieza con el problema, no con tu producto
3. **Hacer pausas** — Deja que los numeros grandes aterricen
4. **No pedir disculpas** — "Somos solo 9 clubs" → "Tenemos 3 clubs que pagaron el ano completo por adelantado"
5. **Terminar con confianza** — No decir "eso es todo", decir "Thank you" y esperar
6. **Practicar 20 veces** — Las primeras 5 van a ser malas. Es normal.
